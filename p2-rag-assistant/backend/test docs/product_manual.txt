NovaSuite 3.0 - User Manual
============================

1. Getting Started
------------------
NovaSuite is a cloud-based project management platform. To get started, navigate to
https://app.novasuite.io and click "Sign Up". Enter your work email address and choose
a password of at least 12 characters. A verification link will be sent to your email.
Click the link to activate your account.

2. Logging In
-------------
Go to https://app.novasuite.io/login. Enter your registered email and password.
If you have forgotten your password, click "Forgot Password" on the login page.
You will receive a password reset email within 5 minutes. The reset link is valid
for 24 hours. After resetting, you will be required to log in again with your new password.

3. Resetting Your Password
--------------------------
To reset your password while logged in:
  a. Click your profile icon in the top-right corner.
  b. Select "Account Settings" from the dropdown.
  c. Click "Change Password" under the Security section.
  d. Enter your current password, then enter and confirm your new password.
  e. Click "Save Changes". You will receive a confirmation email.

If you are locked out after 5 failed login attempts, your account will be locked for
15 minutes. After that, you may try again or use the "Forgot Password" option.

4. Creating a Project
---------------------
From the dashboard, click the "+ New Project" button in the top-left panel.
Enter the project name, description, and due date. Select the team members to invite.
Choose a project template (Kanban, Scrum, or Blank) and click "Create".
Projects can have a maximum of 50 members on the Standard plan and unlimited
members on the Enterprise plan.

5. Managing Tasks
-----------------
Inside a project, click "+ Add Task" to create a new task. Each task can have:
  - A title and description
  - An assignee (one team member)
  - A due date
  - A priority level: Low, Medium, High, or Critical
  - Attachments up to 50 MB each

Tasks can be moved between columns (To Do, In Progress, Done) by dragging and dropping.

6. Notifications
----------------
By default, you will receive email notifications for:
  - Tasks assigned to you
  - Comments on tasks you are watching
  - Project deadline reminders (3 days and 1 day before)

To change notification preferences, go to Account Settings > Notifications.
You can also enable push notifications by installing the NovaSuite mobile app
(available on iOS and Android).

7. Billing and Plans
--------------------
NovaSuite offers three plans:
  - Free: Up to 3 projects, 5 members, 1 GB storage
  - Standard ($12/user/month): Unlimited projects, 50 members per project, 100 GB storage
  - Enterprise ($29/user/month): Unlimited everything, SSO, priority support, audit logs

Billing is monthly or annual (annual gives a 20% discount). To upgrade, go to
Settings > Billing > Upgrade Plan.

8. Contacting Support
---------------------
For technical issues, email support@novasuite.io or use the in-app chat (bottom-right corner).
Support hours are Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM UTC.
Enterprise customers have access to 24/7 priority support.
