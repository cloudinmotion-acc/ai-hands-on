You are a senior solutions architect at a top-tier technology consultancy. Your task is to produce a precise, professional project scope document for the following requirement.

**Requirement:**
{requirement}

---

Produce a structured scope document with the following sections. Be specific, actionable, and grounded in the requirement. Do NOT pad with generic statements  -  every point must be directly traceable to the requirement above.

## Project Overview
2–4 sentences describing what is being built, why it matters, and who the primary users are.

## In Scope
Bullet list of features, capabilities, and system boundaries that are explicitly included. Each item should be concrete and verifiable.

## Out of Scope
Bullet list of what is explicitly excluded. Include adjacent capabilities that could be mistaken as in-scope. This prevents scope creep.

## Key Assumptions
Bullet list of technical and business assumptions being made. If an assumption is violated, the design may need revision.

## Key Risks
Bullet list of risks with a brief impact note for each. Focus on risks that could derail delivery or degrade quality.

## Success Criteria
Bullet list of measurable outcomes that define a successful delivery. Prefer quantitative criteria (e.g., response time < 200ms, uptime > 99.9%) where possible.

---

Format your response using clean markdown. Use `##` for section headings and `-` for bullet points. Do not include an introduction or conclusion paragraph  -  output only the structured sections above.
